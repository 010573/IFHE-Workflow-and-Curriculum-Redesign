# Curriculum Audit and Redesign

## Student Information

Program: B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)

Admission Batch: 2023

Curriculum Version: Not Confirmed

---

# Objective

Review the B.Tech CSE curriculum and compare it with industry requirements in software engineering, artificial intelligence, cloud computing, cybersecurity, and data science.

---

# Methodology

1. Review available IFHE/ICFAI course documents.
2. Analyze industry trends.
3. Compare curriculum with contemporary job requirements.
4. Evaluate AI-assisted learning opportunities.
5. Propose curriculum improvements.

---

# Access Limitation

The complete semester-wise curriculum and credit structure were not available during this analysis.

Therefore:

- Semester mapping remains incomplete.
- Credit calculations are preliminary.
- Recommendations are based on available course documents and industry expectations.

This limitation is documented to avoid presenting assumptions as verified facts.
