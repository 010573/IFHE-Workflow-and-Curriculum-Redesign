# Time and Effort Calculations

## Current Process

Proposal Preparation = 15 min

Faculty Review = 10 min

Student Affairs Review = 10 min

Budget Review = 5 min

Venue Review = 10 min

Communication = 5 min

Total = 55 min

## Rework

20% requests require correction.

Correction effort = 20 min

0.20 × 20 = 4 min

Average effort:

55 + 4 = 59 min/request

## Monthly Effort

10 requests/month

59 × 10 = 590 min

590 ÷ 60 = 9.83 hours/month

## Improved Process

Average Time = 35 min/request

## Time Saved

59 − 35 = 24 min/request

24 × 10 = 240 min/month

240 ÷ 60 = 4 hours/month
